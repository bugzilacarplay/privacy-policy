Bugzila Car Play respects user privacy. The app is designed to play local media, online radio, Android Auto media, and Cast-compatible audio while keeping user data collection to a minimum.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Bugzila Car Play handles information when users install, open, and use the app. The app provides features such as local music playback, local video browsing, online radio streaming, Android Auto media browsing, Google Cast playback, theme settings, audio settings, favorites, backup and restore, notifications, and optional support or diagnostic features.

Bugzila Car Play does not require users to create an account or sign in to use the main app features.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Bugzila Car Play does not intentionally collect the following types of personal information:

  • Name
  • Home address
  • Phone number
  • Personal email address inside the app
  • Precise location or GPS location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • SMS or call logs
  • Advertising ID
  • Payment card information
  • General Google Drive files

3. Local Music and Video Files

Bugzila Car Play may scan and display local music or video files stored on the user's device, depending on the permissions granted by the user. This is used only to allow the user to browse and play their own media files inside the app.

Local music files, video files, and media content remain on the user's device. Bugzila Car Play does not upload local media files to Bugzila servers.

4. Online Radio Streaming

Bugzila Car Play allows users to browse and play online radio stations. Online radio streams may be provided by third-party radio directories, public radio APIs, and individual radio station servers.

When a user plays an online radio station, the device may connect directly or indirectly to third-party streaming servers. Those third-party servers may receive technical information such as:

IP address User agent or device playback request information Time of access Requested stream URL Streaming or connection status Bugzila Car Play does not control the privacy practices of third-party radio stations or streaming providers. Users should review the privacy policies of those third-party services when applicable.

5. Region-Based Radio Suggestions

Bugzila Car Play may use the device language or region setting, such as the Android locale, to show region-based radio suggestions such as “Thailand Hits”, “USA Hits”, or “Japan Hits”.

This feature does not use GPS, precise location, or Android location services. The app does not request location permission for this feature.

6. Android Auto

Bugzila Car Play supports Android Auto media browsing and playback. When using Android Auto, media information such as station name, song title, artist name, album art, playback status, and queue information may be shared with the Android Auto system to display and control media playback in the vehicle.

This information is used only for app functionality and media control. Bugzila Car Play does not use Android Auto data for advertising.

7. Google Cast

Bugzila Car Play may support Google Cast or Cast-compatible playback. When users cast audio to a compatible speaker or display, media information such as title, artist, station name, artwork, stream URL, playback status, and connection status may be sent to the Cast device to enable playback.

Cast features are initiated by the user. When the user disconnects Cast, the app attempts to stop the Cast session and clear related Cast playback metadata.

8. Favorites, Settings, and App Preferences

Bugzila Car Play stores certain app settings locally on the user's device to provide app functionality. This may include:

Favorite radio stations Theme settings EQ and audio preset settings Online radio settings Cast preference settings Notification preferences Android Auto related preferences Playback and display preferences These settings are stored locally and are used to improve the user experience.

9. Backup and Restore

Bugzila Car Play may support optional backup and restore features to help users keep their app settings when changing devices or reinstalling the app.

9.1 Android System Backup

If enabled by the user on their Android device, some Bugzila Car Play app settings may be backed up by the Android system backup service associated with the user's Google Account. This may include configuration data such as:

Favorite radio station information Theme preferences EQ preset settings Online radio settings Cast preference settings Notification preferences Other app configuration preferences Bugzila Car Play does not directly access the user's Google Drive account for Android system backup. The backup process is handled by Android system services according to the user's device and Google Account settings.

Local music files, local video files, and full media content are not uploaded by Bugzila Car Play to Bugzila servers as part of this process.

9.2 Manual Export and Import

Users may choose to manually export their Bugzila Car Play configuration as a backup file, such as a JSON configuration file. Users can choose where to save this file, including local storage, Google Drive, OneDrive, USB storage, or another location supported by Android's file picker.

Manual export and import are user-initiated actions. The app does not automatically share exported backup files with the developer. Users are responsible for where they store, upload, or share their exported backup files.

9.3 Data Included in Backup

Backup data may include:

Favorite radio station information Theme settings EQ preset settings Online radio settings Cast settings preferences Notification preferences App configuration preferences Backup data does not include:

Local music files Local video files Full media file contents General Google Drive files Device identifiers Raw payment card information Diagnostic logs, unless the user explicitly chooses to include them in a support report

10. Notifications

Bugzila Car Play may provide optional mobile notifications for useful app-related information, such as app updates, important bug fixes, feature announcements, or user-enabled alerts.

Users can control notification permissions through Android system settings. If notification settings are provided inside the app, users may enable or disable supported notification categories.

Bugzila Car Play does not use notifications for intrusive advertising.

11. Problem Reports and Diagnostics

Bugzila Car Play may include optional support or diagnostic features. If a user chooses to send feedback or report a problem, the report may include technical information needed to understand and fix the issue.

Optional diagnostic information may include:

App version Android version Device model Playback mode Online radio connection status Cast connection status Android Auto media status Error messages Sanitized diagnostic logs Diagnostic reports are user-initiated and are used for support, troubleshooting, app stability, and bug fixing. Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in support messages.
