# Privacy Policy for Bugzila Car Play

**Effective Date:** 12 June 2026  
**App Name:** Bugzila Car Play  
**Package Name:** `com.bugzilacarplay.app`  
**Developer:** © 2026 Bugzila Labs  
**Privacy Contact:** bugzila@gmail.com  
**Official Privacy Policy URL:** `https://bugzilacarplay.github.io/privacy-policy/`

Bugzila Car Play is designed with a privacy-first approach. The app is built for local media playback, online radio playback, Android Auto media playback, audio settings, and related convenience features. Bugzila Car Play does not sell personal or sensitive user data.

This Privacy Policy explains how Bugzila Car Play accesses, uses, stores, shares, protects, retains, and deletes information when you use the app.

---

## 1. Overview

Bugzila Car Play is a media application that provides:

- Local Music playback
- Phone-only Video playback
- Online Radio playback
- Android Auto media playback
- Audio and equalizer settings
- Favorite stations and playback convenience features
- Optional diagnostics and problem reporting

The app is designed to minimize data collection. Most settings and media information are stored locally on the user's device.

---

## 2. Local Music and Video Access

Bugzila Car Play may request access to audio and video files on your device so that you can play local music and phone-only video content.

This access is used only for app functionality, such as:

- Displaying local songs and videos
- Playing selected media files
- Reading basic media information such as title, artist, album, duration, file type, and artwork when available
- Building local music queues and playback lists

Local media files remain on your device. Bugzila Car Play does not upload your music or video files to Bugzila servers.

If you deny media permissions, some Local Music or Video features may not work.

---

## 3. Online Radio and Third-Party Radio Services

Bugzila Car Play provides Online Radio features by connecting to public radio directory services and third-party radio streams.

When you use Online Radio, the app may connect to:

- Public radio directory APIs such as Radio Browser
- Third-party radio station stream URLs
- Third-party radio station artwork or favicon URLs when available

Bugzila Car Play does not own, host, rebroadcast, or license third-party radio station content. Availability, metadata, stream quality, artwork, and station information are controlled by each radio station or public directory provider.

When connecting to public radio directory services or third-party radio streams, those third-party services may receive standard technical request information, such as:

- IP address
- Device network request information
- User agent or app/network client information
- Access time
- Requested directory API endpoint or stream URL

These services are operated by third parties and are not controlled by Bugzila Car Play. Their own privacy policies and logging practices may apply.

---

## 4. Android Auto

Bugzila Car Play supports Android Auto media playback through Android media session and media library features.

Android Auto may display media information such as:

- Station name
- Song title
- Artist name
- Queue items
- Artwork
- Playback controls

This information is used only to provide media browsing, playback, and control features while using Android Auto.

Bugzila Car Play does not provide phone-only video playback through Android Auto.

---

## 5. Analytics, Crash Diagnostics, and Developer Diagnostics

Bugzila Car Play may include optional diagnostics features to help improve stability and troubleshoot issues.

By default, the following options are turned off:

- Usage Analytics
- Crash Diagnostics
- Developer Diagnostics
- Problem Report Attachment

If enabled by the user, diagnostics may include:

- App version
- Android version
- Device model
- App settings related to playback or diagnostics
- Playback state
- Error messages
- Selected station or media status
- Sanitized technical logs

Diagnostics are used only to improve the app, troubleshoot user-reported problems, and maintain app stability.

Bugzila Car Play does not use diagnostics for advertising or selling user data.

---

## 6. Problem Reports and User Support

Bugzila Car Play may allow users to send feedback or problem reports manually. Problem reports are sent only when the user chooses to report a problem.

A problem report may include:

- App version
- Android version
- Device model
- Playback mode
- Selected station or media status
- Error messages
- Sanitized diagnostic logs

Problem reports should not include personal files, passwords, private messages, payment information, or sensitive personal information.

If users contact Bugzila Car Play through Facebook or another external support channel, the information users choose to provide may also be processed according to that third-party platform's own privacy policy.

---

## 7. Premium Features and Google Play Billing

Bugzila Car Play may offer optional Premium features through Google Play Billing.

Google Play handles payment processing. Bugzila Car Play does not collect or store your credit card number, banking information, or full payment details.

For Premium features, the app may receive or store limited purchase-related information such as:

- Product ID
- Purchase status
- Purchase token
- Entitlement or premium access status

This information is used only to activate, verify, and restore Premium access.

---

## 8. Data Sharing

Bugzila Car Play does not sell personal or sensitive user data.

The app may connect to third-party services only as needed for app functionality, such as:

- Public radio directory services
- Third-party radio stream servers
- Third-party station artwork or favicon servers
- Google Play services for billing and app distribution
- User-selected support or feedback channels

Third-party services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and server logging practices.

---

## 9. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Most app settings are stored locally on your device, including:

- Audio settings
- Equalizer settings
- Theme settings
- Favorite stations
- Radio server settings
- Cache preferences
- Privacy and diagnostics preferences
- Optional local diagnostic reports

Local settings, favorites, cache preferences, privacy settings, and local diagnostic reports are stored on your device until you clear them in the app, clear app data through Android system settings, or uninstall the app.

Where supported, you can clear privacy settings and local diagnostic reports inside the app.

If you send a support or problem report through an external support channel, the report may be retained only as long as needed to investigate, respond to, and resolve the issue. You may contact us to request deletion of support-related information that you previously provided.

Bugzila Car Play does not create user accounts. Therefore, there is no in-app account deletion flow.

---

## 10. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect user data and limit data access to what is needed for app functionality.

Where network communication is required, the app attempts to use secure connections when available. Some public radio streams may still use non-secure HTTP links because they are provided by third-party radio stations.

Users should avoid sending passwords, private files, payment information, or sensitive personal information through support or problem reports.

---

## 11. Ads

Bugzila Car Play does not currently include third-party advertising.

If advertising is added in the future, this Privacy Policy and the app's Google Play Data Safety information will be updated before release.

Bugzila Car Play is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through a support channel, please contact us so we can review and remove it if appropriate.
